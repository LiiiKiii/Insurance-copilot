# Portfolio Quality Excellence Standard 2026

## Purpose

Portfolio Quality Excellence recognises sustainable general-insurance performance rather than production volume alone.

## Recommended Scorecard

| Dimension | Reference level |
|---|---:|
| Quote-to-bind conversion | At least 35% |
| Policy retention | At least 90% |
| Training compliance | All mandatory modules completed |
| Data quality | No unresolved duplicate or missing-key exceptions |

Written premium is reviewed alongside these measures, but no agent should improve volume by weakening suitability, disclosure, or documentation standards.

## Review Rules

- A missing conversion denominator is marked `Data Unavailable`, not 0%.
- Retention uses retained policies divided by the prior in-force portfolio.
- Training completion is synthetic demonstration data and must be identified as such in reports.
- Results are for internal coaching and recognition only.
