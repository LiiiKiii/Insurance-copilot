# AG25011 — Quote-to-Bind Quality Campaign

## Objective

Support the 2026 H1 Conversion Quality Challenge with better discovery, quotation clarity, and follow-up.

## Standard

- Reference target: 35% valid quote-to-bind conversion.
- Respond to qualified leads within four business hours.
- Record lost-business reasons.
- Missing denominators are reported as unavailable.

Pressure selling or incomplete disclosure disqualifies an entry.
