# AG24050 — Commercial Lines Portfolio Review

## Objective

Improve the quality of Commercial portfolios through documented risk reviews.

## Eligible Activity

- Commercial motor, package, liability, crime, transit, machinery, and workers' compensation quotations.
- Each opportunity must have a recorded exposure summary and suitability rationale.
- Written premium is counted in USD only after business is bound.

## Exclusions

Duplicate, test, cancelled, declined, or undocumented cases are excluded.
