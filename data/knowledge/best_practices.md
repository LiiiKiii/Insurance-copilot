# Insurance Sales Best Practices

## Improving Low Average Premium per Case

When an agent's average premium per case is below the company average of HKD 42,000, a common cause is excessive focus on small savings policies.

**Core strategies:**

1. **Needs replay:** Ask, “If you were unable to work for six months starting tomorrow, what would happen to your family finances?” Use the answer to identify the protection gap.
2. **Coverage-based quotation:** Start with the customer's actual protection need. A useful reference is coverage equal to annual income multiplied by ten, plus outstanding liabilities.
3. **Rider combination:** Pair each main policy with at least one relevant rider, such as critical illness or medical coverage. This can raise average premium by approximately 30–40%.
4. **High-value prospect identification:** Prioritise referred prospects with monthly income above HKD 100,000.

**Example:** In 2026 Q1, Chan Chi-ming used needs replay to increase average premium from HKD 28,000 to HKD 35,000, a quarterly increase of 25%.

**Action plan:**

- Ask one protection-gap question in every client meeting.
- Present a protection-focused main policy with one suitable rider by default.
- Aim to raise average premium to at least HKD 35,000 within three months.

## Improving Low Lead Follow-up and Conversion

When lead conversion is below the peer average of 61%, the usual cause is follow-up cadence rather than the product itself.

**Core strategies:**

1. **The 72-hour rule:** Make the first call within 72 hours of receiving a lead. Conversion declines sharply after this period.
2. **Three-contact sequence:**
   - First contact: understand the need and book a meeting.
   - Second contact: send a tailored proposal and confirm the meeting.
   - Third contact: conduct the meeting, provide a quotation, and agree on the next action.
3. **Relationship deposits:** Make at least one helpful, non-sales contact each week, such as a greeting, useful article, or industry update.
4. **Daily follow-up review:** Spend 15 minutes at 09:00 reviewing lead status and keep the day's priority list to five items or fewer.

**Action plan:**

- Maintain a lead tracker with the most recent contact time.
- Reserve 30 minutes at 16:00 each day for follow-up.
- Clear every lead that has had no contact for more than 72 hours this week.

## Final 30-Day Competition Sprint

When fewer than 30 days remain before a competition deadline, switch to sprint mode.

**Three sprint priorities:**

1. **Prioritise high-probability cases:** Start with recent quotations that have not closed. Contact one or two each day and ask which concerns remain unresolved.
2. **Existing-client coverage and referrals:** Review clients from the past two years under the theme of a protection update, then request one referral from each suitable client.
3. **Joint case work:** Cooperate with colleagues whose product strengths complement your own and exchange appropriate prospects.

**Action plan:**

- Update the sprint progress sheet before the Monday morning meeting.
- Record the number of signed cases and client contacts before finishing each day.

## Time Management for an MDRT Sprint

When the MDRT gap is below HKD 50,000, focused execution can close the remaining distance without reducing advice quality.

**Core strategies:**

1. **Focus on high-value clients:** One or two cases above HKD 30,000 may close the gap. Review existing clients affected by a new child, mortgage, promotion, or income increase.
2. **Use the milestone appropriately:** Explain the MDRT milestone honestly to established clients and request introductions where a genuine protection need may exist.
3. **Revisit dormant proposals:** Review quotations from the previous six months, especially clients who asked for more time to consider.
4. **Follow pending policies:** Confirm outstanding underwriting documents and help clients submit them promptly.

**Reminder:** Maintain a calm, professional approach. Urgency must not reduce the quality or suitability of advice.

## When to Discuss Additional Coverage with Existing Clients

Existing-client reviews are a relatively efficient source of appropriate new business.

**Useful review moments:**

1. **Two or three months before a policy anniversary:** Offer an annual protection review.
2. **Major life changes:**
   - marriage or childbirth: review life and family protection;
   - a home purchase or mortgage: review coverage against the outstanding mortgage;
   - promotion or salary increase: align protection with income growth;
   - ageing parents: review relevant medical protection.
3. **Rising medical costs:** Review whether existing medical cover still meets expected expenses.
4. **Market volatility:** Some savings-focused clients may wish to reassess protection needs.

**Example opening:**

“It has been [X] years since your policy began, and your family circumstances and income may have changed. I would like to offer a complimentary ten-minute protection review to check for any gaps. When would be convenient this week?”
