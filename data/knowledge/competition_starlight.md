# 2026 H1 Conversion Quality Challenge

## Rule Summary

- Competition ID: `COMP_CONVERSION_2026H1`
- Metric: bound policies divided by valid quotations or submissions
- Target: 35%
- Deadline: 30 June 2026
- Reward: advanced needs-analysis workshop place

Only records with a valid denominator are evaluated. Missing denominators remain unavailable. Duplicate quotations, test records, and cancelled submissions are excluded.

Recommended actions include prompt first response, structured follow-up, clear coverage comparisons, and accurate quotation documentation. Agents must not pressure customers or omit material terms to improve conversion.
