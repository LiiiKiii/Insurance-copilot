# 2026 New Business Commission Excellence

## Rule Summary

- Honour ID: `HONOR_NEW_BUSINESS_COMMISSION_2026`
- Metric: new-business premium multiplied by the documented product commission assumption
- Target: USD 50,000
- Deadline: 31 December 2026
- Reward: internal recognition and mentoring programme

Commission is a derived project metric. Product assumptions vary by general-insurance line and are documented in the seed data. Reversed, cancelled, or declined business is excluded.

This internal metric is not a payroll statement and must not be presented as actual compensation owed to an agent.
