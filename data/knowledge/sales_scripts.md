# General Insurance Customer Conversation Scripts

## Handle an Expensive Product Objection

> "I understand price is important. Let us compare the limits, deductible, exclusions, and services side by side so you can see what changes if we reduce the premium. Which part matters most to you: keeping the monthly cost down, limiting out-of-pocket loss, or preserving broader cover?"

Do not imply that the most expensive option is automatically best.

## Risk and Needs Analysis

> "Before I recommend an option, may I confirm what needs to be insured, how it is used, the maximum loss you could comfortably absorb, and any recent changes or claims?"

For commercial clients, also confirm revenue, payroll, locations, vehicle use, contractual liabilities, and business interruption exposure where relevant.

## Referral Request

> "If this review was useful, is there a colleague or friend who would also benefit from a no-obligation insurance review? I will explain the options clearly and will not contact them without permission."

## Existing-Client Cross-Line Review

> "Your current policy covers [existing exposure]. Since the last review, have you added a vehicle, property, equipment, employees, or a new business activity? If so, we can check whether there is an uncovered exposure."

## Renewal Conversation

> "Your policy renews on [date]. Before comparing renewal options, let us confirm whether the insured assets, drivers, operations, limits, and deductibles are still accurate. I will highlight every material change from the current policy."

## Closing Without Pressure

> "Based on the coverage and cost we reviewed, which option best matches your priorities? If anything is unclear, we should resolve it before you decide."
